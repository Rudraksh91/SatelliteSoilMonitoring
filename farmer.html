Before you start

To give advice for your farm, this page sends the approximate location of your farm to three free public services: Open-Meteo (weather), ISRIC SoilGrids (soil type) and AWS / Copernicus Sentinel-2 (satellite pictures).

Your farm outline, name and notes are kept only on this phone. We run no server and ask for no account. You can delete a farm any time (“Delete farm”).

Advice is an estimate, not a guarantee. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may withdraw at any time by deleting your farms and clearing this site's data.
